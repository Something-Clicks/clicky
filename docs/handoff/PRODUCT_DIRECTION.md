# Product & Business Direction

## Positioning
Something Clicks is a software-first AI marketing platform for small business owners. It helps owners understand what is happening in their marketing, decide what to do next, prepare useful work with AI, approve actions, and see what happened.

**Public line:** Make the complicated click.
**Internal compass:** Make marketing easy.
**Brand promise:** Understand your marketing. Know what to do next. See the work get done.

## The customer problem
Owners are experts at delivering their product or service, not necessarily marketing. Existing options can leave them with opaque agency retainers, complex tools, scattered advice, and dashboards without clear next steps.

## Product method
Connect → Analyze → Recommend → Execute → Measure.

1. Connect: Add the website and optionally authorize supported data sources.
2. Analyze: Evaluate available evidence and explain what it shows.
3. Recommend: Turn findings into prioritized Action Cards.
4. Execute: Prepare drafts and tasks; request approval where required.
5. Measure: Show what was completed and what changed using verified data.

## Launch scope
- Website analysis using safe fetching and Claude server-side.
- Optional customer-authorized Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, and Google Business Profile connections.
- Action Cards with evidence, impact, confidence, recommended next step, approval requirements, status, and result.
- AI-prepared content and marketing tasks for approval and copy/download.
- Subscription and one-time audit checkout using Stripe test mode first.
- Customer settings, business profile, data connections, activity history, approvals, and billing.

## Out of scope until separately approved
- Unrestricted autonomous agents.
- Unverified auto-publishing integrations.
- Semrush API integration.
- A native mobile app.
- Agency CRM, account-manager assignment, proposals, team inbox, or staff delivery operations as core product features.
- Any new paid vendor or subscription.

## Commercial working prices
Diagnostic Audit $39 one-time; Starter AI $49/month; Growth AI $149/month; Scale & Automation $399/month. Keep these in a single typed configuration. Stripe test mode first. These are product configuration values, not claims of proven willingness to pay.
