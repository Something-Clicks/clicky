# Clicky — Mascot Brief

## Role
Clicky is Something Clicks' original emoji-style mascot. The character represents the “aha!” moment: marketing suddenly makes sense and the next step becomes clear.

## Name and relationship
- Company: Something Clicks.
- Public line: Make the complicated click.
- Mascot nickname: Clicky.
- Clicky is not a company rename, product tier, or separate business.

## Character direction
- The face must remain warm yellow so it is immediately understood as an emoji.
- Expressive, happy, clever, welcoming, and recognizable at tiny sizes.
- Distinctive eyes/mouth and silhouette; keep the shape simple.
- Avoid the generic lightbulb, graphs, cursors, robot heads, gears, and piles of decorative rays.
- Use sparingly in onboarding, empty states, helper tips, celebrations, and selected marketing touchpoints.
- Do not put Clicky on every screen or let the mascot distract from the task.

## Color relationship
Clicky yellow `#FFC928` is the character signature. Cobalt blue `#3977D5` is the product's primary interface color. Coral `#FF897C`, mint `#75DDB5`, and navy `#252A35` support the identity.

## Asset usage
`assets/clicky-emoji.png` is the latest generated concept reference. It is not a final vector master. Use it to guide the character's face, color, and personality. Recreate a clean scalable SVG or equivalent only after checking the visual in the reference. Preserve a raster fallback. Do not invent a new mascot style that conflicts with the supplied concept.
